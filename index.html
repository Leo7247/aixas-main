03 // Specialized Inspection Modules

Built for Complex Manufacturing Lines

Configure, calibrate, and deploy specialized inspection routines in a few clicks—from electronics assembly to automotive dispensing and pharmaceutical packaging.

MODULE // 01

PCBA & Conformal Coating

Verify SMT and through-hole presence, polarity, position shift, solder defects, and foreign debris. Perform fiducial alignment under visible light and coating coverage verification under UV illumination.

MODULE // 02

Sealant & Adhesive Bead Inspection

Train complex dispense paths in minutes. Continuously inspect bead width, path deviation, coverage consistency, material splash, and microscopic gaps or voids in real time.

MODULE // 03

Edge Profile & Flaw Detection

Extract lines, corners, and circles with sub-pixel edge-finding algorithms that quantify micro-deviations across metal cans, stamped parts, and painted interfaces.

MODULE // 04

Print, Label & Surface Defects

Detect label print errors, surface scratches, foreign particles, and pharmaceutical pill defects across high-throughput packaging lines.

MODULE // 05

AI OCR & Traceability

Read 1D barcodes, Data Matrix, QR, and PDF417 codes on curved or reflective surfaces—plus dot-matrix expiry dates and laser-etched serials via an editable user character library.

MODULE // 06

2D Dimensional Metrology

Guided Caliper and geometric measurement routines compute point-to-point distances, parallel gaps, 3- and 4-point angles, and circle radii calibrated to mm and μm.

04 // Hardware & Robotics Control

Direct Motion & Robot Synchronization.

Eliminate complex middleware. X-Vision embeds hardware communication directly inside the vision platform to drive multi-axis stages, strobe lighting controllers, and industrial robot arms.

Automated Optical Inspection (AOI) Stations

Embedded multi-axis motion control and multi-channel LED strobe lighting control make building complete multi-angle AOI stations seamless.

Vision-Guided Robotics (VGR)

Automatic camera-to-robot hand-eye coordinate calibration for 4-axis and 6-axis industrial robots—enabling repeatable pick-and-place, precision alignment, and automated screw fastening.
